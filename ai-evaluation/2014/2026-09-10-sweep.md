## Survivor evidence digest: malicious IdPs

**Original artifact:** [arXiv v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.1623v1), retrieved as `/tmp/missed-2014/idp.pdf`, full text `/tmp/missed-2014/idp.txt`. Version record confirms December 4, 2014; no later version's 12/17 result or revised two-class taxonomy is used. Some component advisories appeared earlier in 2014; exact technical cutoff within that year needs the final review to distinguish bare fixes from detailed disclosure.

**Source-read result:** a programmable hostile OpenID provider calibrates against honest requests, mutates protocol parameters, and replays recorded attack profiles. Authors report compromising 11 of 16 implementations, including eight without victim interaction. Signature validation alone fails when issuer, key handle, returned identity and discovery identity disagree. The useful residual contribution is a testing workflow and particular validation-bypass compositions, not a new discovery that IdPs can be hostile.

**Contribution comparisons:**
