# TECAPI source discovery and follow-up — 11 September 2026

Completed the bounded discovery, judging and preservation follow-up requested for
TECAPI. Four new techniques qualify for the historical missed-work lists. An
earlier paper expands one existing entry. Three fully scored candidates do not
qualify; two additional leads were resolved during screening or held for evidence.
Source absence alone was never treated as technique novelty.

## Coverage

- The live [catalogue](http://www.tecapi.com/public/relative-vulnerability-rating-gui.jsp)
  responded over HTTP; HTTPS timed out.
- Retrieved all 288 linked attack-vector detail pages, with no fetch failures.
- Extracted 1,120 distinct reference URLs after basic normalization (scheme, `www`,
  URL decoding, case and trailing slash). This is a discovery count, not a document
  deduplication result. Navigation/social links were excluded.
- The [reference inventory](2026-09-11-tecapi-references.tsv) preserves literal URLs
  and their originating vector IDs/names. Detail URLs have the form
  `http://www.tecapi.com/public/rvr-view-attack-vector-gui.jsp?attackVectorId=39`.
- Compared leads with root `20*.md` lists, including `2016-17.md` and `2026-ai.md`,
  and searched existing evaluation notes. Checked selected titles, aliases and
  mechanisms beyond exact URL matching. Did not individually read or adjudicate
  every reference in the inventory.
- Fully evaluated eight credible candidates, using complete primary documents,
  dated local and external prior art, publication-time scores and a second
  verification pass. Diviner was an additional lead found while following the
  session-puzzling sources.

## Decisions

Historical inclusion requires **at least 55/100 and a qualifying originality,
extension, combination or tooling/methodology verdict**. A useful case study does
not become a separate missed technique merely by passing the numerical threshold.
The original community nominations and rankings are unchanged.
