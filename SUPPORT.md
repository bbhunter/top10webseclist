## ⭐ Star it — the free one that genuinely helps

If this archive has saved you a search, settled an argument about who did something
first, or handed you a dead link's rescued copy:

### 👉 **[Star the repository](https://github.com/irsdl/top10webseclist)**

Stars are how researchers find this. Every one of them puts two decades of preserved
research in front of someone who did not know it survived. It costs nothing and takes
one click — it is the single most useful thing most people can do here.

Sharing works just as well: post it, link it in a writeup, or send it to someone hunting
for prior art.

## 💛 Sponsor the preservation work

Preservation is the expensive part. Capturing a year's citations means chasing dead
domains through the Wayback Machine, re-rendering pages that fight headless browsers,
verifying that what came back is the *right* document rather than a consent wall, and
storing the result twice — as readable Markdown and as a PDF of the page as published.

### 👉 **[Sponsor on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/irsdl)**

Sponsorship goes toward keeping that going:

| What it covers | Why it costs something |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **Hosting** | `webhacklist.com`, its domain renewal, and serving a growing archive of PDFs |
| 🗄️ **Preservation** | Recapturing broken or faulty snapshots, and re-chasing links as hosts die |
| 🔧 **Tooling** | The capture, validation and translation pipeline behind `archived-references/` |
| 🕰️ **Time** | Curating each year by hand — the lists are not scraped, they are checked |

One-off and recurring sponsorships are both welcome, and any amount is genuinely useful.
Nothing here is paywalled and nothing ever will be: the archive stays free, complete and
open regardless of whether anyone sponsors it.

> [!NOTE]
> Sponsorship buys no influence over the lists. Entries are added on the evidence —
> a nomination in that year's official round, or a review-gated audit find — and no
> amount of money puts research on a list or takes it off one.

## 🤝 Or contribute instead

Time is worth as much as money here, and often more:

- **Fix a dead link** — swap in the author's new domain or a Wayback Machine capture.
- **Propose a missed technique** — the official nomination rounds sometimes overlooked
  research. If it qualifies as a web hacking technique nominee for that year, open an
  issue or PR with its original source and it will be reviewed for addition.
- **Report a bad capture** — a PDF that rendered a consent wall, a 404 or a truncated
  page. Say which file, and it gets filed for recapture.
- **Improve the site** — [`website/`](website/) is dependency-free static HTML, CSS and
  JavaScript.

See [Contributing](README.md#contributing) for the details.
